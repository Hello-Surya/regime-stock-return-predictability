\section{Economic Value}

The economic-value analysis is the next empirical stage of the research. For each out-of-sample month, stocks are ranked by predicted next-month return separately for Elastic Net and XGBoost. The planned baseline forms prediction deciles and evaluates the return difference between the highest-prediction and lowest-prediction deciles, $D10-D1$.

The baseline portfolio analysis reports equal-weighted results first and then value-weighted results. Performance is evaluated for the full sample and separately in HIGH- and LOW-VIX months. Turnover and the frozen transaction-cost robustness specification of 0 and 50 basis points one way are added after the initial portfolio construction is validated.

No portfolio results are reported in the current manuscript version because the single-stock out-of-sample validation is completed before the cross-sectional economic-value stage. This ordering prevents the research from interpreting single-stock forecast metrics as evidence of profitable cross-sectional ranking ability.

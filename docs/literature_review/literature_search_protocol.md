# Reproducible Literature Search Protocol — Milestone 11

- **Date:** 2026-10-20 (America/New_York).
- **Sources consulted:** Web-discoverable publisher metadata and abstracts (Oxford Academic, Wiley, Elsevier/ScienceDirect, INFORMS, Annual Reviews), NBER, SSRN, RePEc/IDEAS, university research profiles, Crossref/DOI landing pages, JSTOR.
- **Search themes:** cross-sectional stock returns + machine learning; Elastic Net/XGBoost/tree forecasting; VIX + volatility states + return predictability; regime-dependent signals; microcaps + trading costs + turnover; forecast comparisons; HAC and multiple testing; publication-version tracking.
- **Selection:** direct overlap on US individual stocks; rigorous adjacent aggregate-return/volatility methods; foundational characteristic models; portfolio implementability and inference; at least one recent competing working paper.
- **Exclusions:** untraceable sources, unsupported claims, duplicate preprints where the journal version exists, market commentary and pure trading tutorials. Bonds included only for documented leakage-correction caution; index high-frequency working paper marked as distinct.
- **Verification rule:** title, author, journal, year, and DOI/verified stable URL from credible landing page. **Abstract verified does not mean full text reviewed**. Missing exact samples and estimator details are flagged in the matrix.
- **Results:** 38 identified and bibliographically checked records; 1 non-refereed working paper; 37 published records (including a corrigendum and surveys); 27 priority analyses in literature_matrix.md.
- **Limitations:** web discovery is not an exhaustive systematic database harvest; no guaranteed complete Google Scholar or licensed full-text access; publisher metadata describes research but rarely supplies complete data-design details. DOI identifiers used here match the displayed source metadata. A second author-supervised full-text and citation-support audit remains mandatory before journal submission.
